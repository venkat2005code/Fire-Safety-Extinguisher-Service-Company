Know Your Equipment

Types of Fire Extinguishers

Using the wrong extinguisher on the wrong fire class is dangerous. Our experts help you choose the right type for every zone in your facility.

Class A

Water / Foam

For ordinary combustibles — wood, paper, cloth, plastics. The most common type in offices and residential settings.

Offices Residential Schools
Class B

CO₂ / Dry Chemical

For flammable liquids and gases — oil, gasoline, paint, propane. Essential for workshops, garages, and service areas.

Factories Garages Labs
Class C

CO₂ / Dry Chemical

For energized electrical equipment — servers, wiring, motors. Never use water on Class C fires — it's lethal.

Data Centers Telecom Utilities
Class D

Dry Powder (Specialist)

For combustible metals — magnesium, titanium, sodium. Required in specific manufacturing and research environments.

R&D Labs Manufacturing
Class K

Wet Chemical (Kitchen)

Specifically engineered for commercial kitchen fires — cooking oils, fats, and greases at high temperatures. NFPA 96 required.

Restaurants Hotels Catering
ABC Multi

ABC Dry Chemical

The most versatile extinguisher, covering Classes A, B, and C fires. The go-to solution for general commercial and industrial use.

Warehouses Retail Commercial

Not sure which extinguisher your facility needs? Our certified experts will assess your risk profile and recommend the right mix.
