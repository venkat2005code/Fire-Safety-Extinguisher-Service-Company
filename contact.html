FAQ

How often do fire extinguishers need inspection?

According to NFPA 10, fire extinguishers require a quick visual inspection monthly, a professional maintenance inspection annually, and internal maintenance/hydrostatic testing every 5 to 12 years depending on the type.

Do you provide emergency after-hours support?

Yes, absolutely. We run a 24/7 emergency dispatch center. If a fire alarm panel goes down or a suppression system discharges, our technicians can be deployed to your location immediately.

Do you service residential homes?

Yes, we have a dedicated residential division that installs hardwired smoke detectors, home fire extinguishers, and helps families develop effective escape routes.
